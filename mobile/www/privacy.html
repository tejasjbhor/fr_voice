Privacy note

This is a field-test product. It is not a hospital record system. Read this before you register.

We store the name, phone number, language, and call time you type in. After a live call we store a transcript, optional WAV in the server’s local recordings/ folder, and health notes the model extracted from what was said.

Family (the Subscriber) can see check-ins, transcripts, and recordings for people they added. Login uses a phone code. Keep the phone in your control.

We do not sell this data. We do not send it to advertisers. Voice vendors (Sarvam, ElevenLabs, Twilio, etc.) process audio to make the call work.

You can delete the account from Profile. That stops future calls. Old transcripts may remain until an operator purges the database.

Ambulance 108 is a call you place yourself. Saathi SOS rings the family number via the same voice bot — it is not a government emergency service.
